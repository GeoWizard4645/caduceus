Tools to compare

Tick what you'd actually cancel
$10/mo

Usually: Raycast Pro

The palette itself, the app launcher, the calculator, your own shortcuts, and window snapping — all built in and free here.

Partial: Raycast's AI Commands are its own paid tier; here AI chat is whatever backend you point it at (including fully local), not a bundled model.

$8/mo

Usually: Superwhisper or Wispr Flow

Push-to-talk dictation with live text as you talk, through Apple's on-device speech recognition. No cloud unless you add one.

Partial: those apps run your words through an LLM afterwards to strip filler words and auto-format. Here the transcript is raw; cleaning it up is a separate rewrite command, not automatic.

$10/mo

Usually: Granola or Otter

Records both sides of a call — the room through system audio, you through the mic — transcribes on-device, and keeps your notes beside it.

Partial: no automatic AI summary or action items. You get the transcript and whatever you typed; summarising it is one more (manual) step, not something that happens for you.

$3/mo

Usually: an app like Paste

Searchable history of text, images and file paths, pinning, per-app exclusions, and optional encryption at rest — all local.

Partial: this Mac only. Apps like Paste sync history to your iPhone and iPad too; Caduceus doesn't leave the machine at all, on purpose.

$10 once

Usually: Rectangle Pro or Moom

Halves, thirds, quarters, sixths, custom nudges, move-to-next-display — over 50 layouts, typed or searched from the same bar as everything else.

Partial: no mouse-drag snap zones. Every placement here is a command, not a drag to a screen edge.

What's deliberately not on this list

PDF reading with AI question-answering isn't priced into this list — it's a real, shipped feature (open Documents from the palette, drop in a PDF, and summarise it or ask it a question), but there's no single paid product it maps cleanly onto the way the five things above do, so no line item was invented for one. Caduceus reads and summarises a web page the same way (see All features), which is why that isn't on this list as a distinct subscription either.

Where the prices came from

Where these prices came from

Checked directly against each product's own pricing page. Monthly, list price, no annual discount applied — the honest, most expensive reading:

  • Raycast Pro — $10/mo billed monthly ($8/mo billed annually).
  • Superwhisper Pro — $8.49/mo billed monthly, rounded down to $8 here. Wispr Flow Pro runs $12–15/mo, i.e. this line is the cheaper of the two examples it names.
  • Otter.ai Pro — $16.99/mo billed monthly ($8.33/mo annually). Granola Business is $14/mo. $10 sits below both list prices, in line with what most people actually land on paying annually.
  • Paste (clipboard manager) — $3.99/mo billed monthly, or $29.99/yr (≈$2.50/mo). $3/mo is the rounded middle.
  • Rectangle Pro — $9.99 one-time. Moom is $15 one-time (with a year of updates). $10 represents the cheaper of the two, and it's a one-time cost either way, not a subscription.

If any of these move, the honest fix is to update the number here, not to keep an old one that flatters the total.
